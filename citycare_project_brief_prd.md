# Project Brief: CityCare Hospital Integration App

## 1. Project Overview
CityCare is a city-wide healthcare integration platform designed to provide citizens with seamless access to hospital directories, medical profiles, and appointment scheduling. The application focuses on reducing the friction between patients and healthcare providers through an intuitive, trust-focused interface.

## 2. Objectives
- **Centralize Information:** Provide a unified directory of all city hospitals and clinics.
- **Simplify Scheduling:** Create a frictionless booking flow for doctors and services.
- **Enhance Navigation:** Integrate map-based search to help users find care near their location.
- **Establish Trust:** Utilize a professional, clean design system that conveys reliability and clinical excellence.

## 3. Target Audience
- Patients seeking specialized medical care or general practitioners.
- Users needing immediate information on ER wait times and hospital proximity.
- Healthcare administrators looking to provide a modern digital entry point for their facilities.

## 4. Key Features & Screens
- **Hospital Directory (Current):** Searchable list with quick filters (Emergency, Specialty) and distance metrics.
- **Map View (Current):** Interactive city map with branded pins and contextual hospital preview cards.
- **Hospital Profile (Current):** Comprehensive view of services, ER wait times, ratings, and contact info.
- **Booking Flow (Current):** Multi-step selection of specialists and appointment time slots.
- **Patient Dashboard (Proposed):** Overview of upcoming appointments, medical records, and prescriptions.

## 5. Design System: "Clinical Nexus"
- **Color Palette:**
  - Primary: Deep Clinical Blue (#2a5c82) for trust and authority.
  - Accent: Healthcare Green for positive action (Booking, Confirmation).
  - Backgrounds: Soft, high-contrast light theme to minimize cognitive load.
- **Typography:** Inter (Sans-serif) for maximum legibility across all age groups.
- **Visual Style:** Rounded corners (8px), generous whitespace, and a flat/minimalist component library.

## 6. Success Metrics
- **Booking Conversion:** Percentage of users who complete the booking flow after selecting a doctor.
- **Search Efficiency:** Time taken for a user to find a specific specialty or hospital.
- **User Trust:** Qualitative feedback on the "Clinical Nexus" visual identity and ease of use.

## 7. Next Steps & Roadmap
- Design the "My Appointments" tracking dashboard.
- Develop the Patient Records/History portal.
- Refine the interactive "Filters" drawer for map-based searches.
- Create a notification system for appointment reminders and emergency alerts.

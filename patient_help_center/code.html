Frequently Asked Questions

How do I access my laboratory test results? expand_more
Log in to your Patient Portal dashboard and navigate to the 'Reports' section. Results are typically released 24-48 hours after processing, though some specialized tests may take longer. You will receive an automated notification when your results are ready for review.
Can I pay my medical bill through the mobile app? expand_more
Yes, the CityCare mobile app supports secure payments via credit card, HSA/FSA cards, and Apple/Google Pay. You can also view your full billing history and download itemized statements for insurance reimbursement.
What is the process for requesting a prescription refill? expand_more
You can request refills directly through the 'Pharmacy' tab in your portal. Please allow 2 business days for your provider to review and approve the request. For urgent refills, please contact your pharmacy directly as they can often send a priority request to our office.
